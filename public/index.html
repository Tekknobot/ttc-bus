What am I seeing?
  • Location is required. Allow location (or pass ?pin=LAT,LON) so the app can pick the nearest TTC stop and show arrivals for that stop.
  • Arrives in shows minutes until a vehicle reaches that stop. Green ≤ 3, Amber ≤ 7, then grey.
  • No route numbers shown. Each row focuses on the destination (headsign) and a second line that highlights the location:
    • At stop #1234 — Dundas St West (the prediction’s stop and primary street), or
    • Nearby vehicle — 120 metres away when no predictions match; this is a proximity fallback within ~400 metres.
  • Opposite platforms included. Sibling stops within about 120 metres are included (usually the opposite side).
  • Auto refresh. The list updates roughly every 10 seconds. Tap Refresh to update immediately.
  • Optional route filtering. You can filter results with ?route=NUMBER (e.g., ?route=72), but the route isn’t displayed in the UI.
  • Privacy. Your location stays in your browser. It’s only used to choose the nearest stop and measure distances.
