What am I seeing?
  • Location is required. When you allow location, the app finds the nearest TTC stop to where you’re standing and shows arrivals for that stop.
  • Arrives in shows how many minutes until a vehicle reaches that stop. Green ≤ 3, Amber ≤ 7, then grey.
  • Route is the route number serving the prediction (e.g., 29, 31, 72).
  • Under each row you’ll see either:
    • “At stop #1234” — the stop the prediction is for, or
    • “Nearby vehicle — 120 metres away” — shown when no predictions match; we fall back to vehicles within ~400 metres of you.
  • Opposite platforms included. We also include “sibling” stops within ~120 metres (often the opposite side of the street) so you won’t miss the other direction.
  • Auto-refresh. The list refreshes roughly every 10 seconds. Tap Refresh anytime to update immediately.
  • Privacy. Your location stays in your browser; it’s used only to pick the nearest stop and measure distances to nearby vehicles.
  • Troubleshooting.
    • If you see “Location error”, enable location for this site in your browser settings and reload.
    • If “No predictions” appears, it usually means the feed hasn’t posted times for this stop yet—try Refresh, or check again in a minute.
